# Philippines v15 model structure

The model is a whole-country CLEWs case for 2020-2053 with four seasonal/daypart
timeslices. Its inherited base contains the linked energy, land, agriculture,
water and emissions systems generated by CLEWs Global and imported into MUIO.
The cumulative source and parameter map is in `../data_sources/MODEL_MAP.csv`.

The v13 layer added environmental-emission calibration. The v14 layer added
power-stock turnover and 3,253 recorded parameter-cell changes. The v15 layer
rebases national precipitation to the ERA5 1991-2020 normal, applies an SSP2-4.5
ensemble-median precipitation path, connects `DEMAGRGWTPHL` to raw groundwater,
and adds the `WATER_SUR_AVAIL` and `WATER_GWT_POTENTIAL` annual constraints.

Water is modeled as national annual potential flow, not as basin allocation or
aquifer stock. The exact equations, technologies, commodities, modes and units
are documented in `../data_sources/calculation_notes/national_water_v15.md`.
