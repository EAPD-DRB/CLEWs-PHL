## Current representation

The retained portable case is `../muio/Philippines_v17_v17.0.0_MUIO.zip`.
It contains the complete current source case. Version 17 inherits every v16
source, ledger record, and evidence artifact, then adds the PSA/NAMRIA 2020
national land-cover partition, annual national land equality, and endogenous
idle/fallow cropland on existing `LNDOTHTOT` mode 2. It also carries a national
policy-rate forest expansion envelope, a reconciled true-grassland reserve,
fixed non-convertible classes, a 2020 total-cropland floor and zero unallocated
land. The underlying model is the
v14 stock-turnover model plus a national precipitation pathway, a corrected
groundwater input link, and annual surface-water and renewable-groundwater
potential-flow ceilings. The final model is a MUIO evolution; `../config/` and
`../model/inputs/` retain the exact upstream raw base from which that lineage
began. Later changes are recorded cumulatively in the same six ledgers and in
the current archive, not by references to an earlier package. The v17 archive
contains the complete editable case but excludes generated solver inputs and
results; those are regenerated by MUIOGO.

The current energy layer removes three misleading `_T1` labels while
preserving stable IDs, uses a 28.4738026159% NREL capacity-weighted offshore
wind annual capacity factor, restores geothermal availability to 0.9 and sets
the onshore wind screened-resource ceiling to 663.84 PJ/year. These are
physical inputs; renewable construction and dispatch remain endogenous.

The 2020 land account is forest 72.3194, grassland/woodland 77.7456,
other-agricultural 2.2879, barren 1.5950, built-up 10.2649, water 6.3198,
cropland 125.2805, and unallocated zero, all in thousand km2. These are
base-year initialization equalities. Forest `VC=-10` is unchanged; transition
limits now use the documented cumulative national envelope. Parcel-level gross
transitions, restoration/conversion costs or lags, and benefit sensitivities
remain deferred.
