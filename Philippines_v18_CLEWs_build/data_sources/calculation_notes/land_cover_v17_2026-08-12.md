# Philippines v17 land-cover calculation record

This note records the calculation boundary behind
`../snapshots/land_cover_2020.json`; that JSON is the authoritative
machine-readable input register.

1. Transcribe PSA/NAMRIA Table 1, 2020 closing stock, including all 13 published
   classes.
2. Exclude the 317.3 km2 `Sea and ocean` class from the terrestrial model
   boundary. Published land after exclusion is 295,566.4 km2.
3. Map Annual crop and Perennial crop to Cropland; the three forest classes to
   Forest; Grassland plus Brush/Shrubs to Grassland and woodland; Open/Barren to
   Barren; Built-up to Built-up; Inland Water plus Marshland/Swamp to Water
   bodies; and Fishpond to Other agricultural land.
4. Multiply the seven pre-reconciliation aggregates by
   `295813.1 / 295566.4 = 1.0008346686226852`.
5. Record model inputs to 0.1 km2 and close the final 0.1 km2 rounding difference
   between Cropland and Water bodies. The result totals exactly 295,813.1 km2.
6. Convert km2 to model units by dividing by 1,000.

The observed class equalities initialize 2020. Annual `MINLNDTOT` equality
retains the national area in every year. Future limits are calculated from
`../snapshots/land_transition_safeguards.json`:

1. `1.5 million ha / 6 / 100000 = 2.5 thousand km2/year` policy-rate proxy.
2. Reconciled true-grass reserve:
   `19610.0 * 1.0008346686226852 / 1000 = 19.6264 thousand km2`.
3. Mapped brush/shrub pool:
   `77.7456 - 19.6264 = 58.1192 thousand km2`.
4. Forest-eligible brush/shrub pool:
   `58.1192 * 0.80 = 46.49536 thousand km2`, using the DENR/MPFD
   production-plantation suitability screen reported by FAO/FMB.
5. `released[y] = min(2.5 * (y - 2020), 46.49536)`.
6. Maximum forest is `72.3194 + 46.49536 = 118.81476 thousand km2`.
7. Forest lower is 72.3194 and upper is `72.3194 + released[y]`.
8. Grass/brush lower is the 19.6264 true-grass reserve and upper is its
   77.7456 base stock. The forest eligibility screen does not restrict brush
   conversion to cropland or built-up land.
9. Built-up follows `10.2649 * population[y]/population[2020] *
   urban_share[y]/0.54`, where the PSA Scenario 2 population path is retained
   and urban share rises linearly from 0.54 in 2020 to 0.65 in 2050.
10. Other/fishpond, barren and water remain at their 2020 stocks;
   cropland remains at least 125.2805; unallocated has a 0.000001-thousand-km2
   upper sentinel because MUIOGO omits numeric-zero activity bounds.

Idle/fallow cropland is calculated endogenously as total cropland less
productive crop activity; it is not an independently observed series. The
2020 balance is `125.2805 - 119.9928 = 5.2877 thousand km2`; that observed
balance is also the all-year upper bound on `LNDOTHTOT` mode 2, preventing the
bookkeeping route from clearing brush solely to create new idle cropland. The
policy-rate value is a planning proxy, and the national envelope is not a gross
parcel-level transition equation.
