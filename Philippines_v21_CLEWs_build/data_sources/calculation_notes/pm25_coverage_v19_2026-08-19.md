# Philippines v19 PM2.5 coverage calculation note

## Decision and scope

Philippines v19 is a parameter-only successor to v18. It adds endogenous
PM2.5 emission factors to existing technologies whose model activities can be
matched defensibly to an authoritative factor. It does not add CO2e work,
exogenous emissions, historical validation, technologies, commodities,
constraints, costs, demands, or emission definitions.

The source case already contained the `PM2_5` emission and emission accounting.
The implementation therefore changes only technology-to-emission membership in
`genData.json` and `EmissionActivityRatio`/companion zero rows in `RYTEM.json`.

The exact inherited baseline is v18.0.1 from Git commit `2735feb`, pulled at
10:09:18 EDT on 2026-08-19. Its result-free archive SHA-256 is
`c3f4ee25d2e8c3315ced1be4bf819673859be45079536abb2cfbc40a65d1dc55`.
This deliberately excludes the later experiment that extended new deployment
caps into the historical 2020-2025 interval.

## Source selection

The primary evidence is the EMEP/EEA air pollutant emission inventory
guidebook: the 2023 chapters for energy industries, industrial combustion,
small combustion, non-road mobile machinery, and coal mining/handling, plus
the 2025 road non-exhaust update. These are established Tier 1 inventory
methods with explicit PM2.5 factors and uncertainty ranges. No complete
Philippines-specific factor set was found that resolves the model's aggregate
fuel/device/control/fleet categories, so the EEA central factors are used as a
transparent fallback rather than presented as locally calibrated values.

All six source PDFs are retained byte-for-byte under `evidence/pm25_v19/`.
`SOURCE_MANIFEST.csv` records URLs, access date, sizes, and SHA-256 hashes.
`FACTOR_SELECTION.csv` records all 23 selected source rows, exact table/page
locators, published uncertainty bounds, model applications, and boundaries.

## Equations and units

For combustion activities measured in PJ of useful activity:

`EAR[y] = EF[g/GJ fuel] × IAR[y][PJ fuel/PJ activity] × 0.001`

because 1 g/GJ equals 0.001 kt/PJ. The model's endogenous annual input-activity
ratios are retained, so efficiency changes propagate into PM2.5 intensity.

Installed Tier 1 combustion factors are:

- industry process heat: oil 20, gas 0.78, coal 108, biomass 140 g/GJ input;
- services heat: oil 18, gas 0.78, coal 108, biomass 160 g/GJ input;
- agriculture stationary heat: coal 108 and biomass 160 g/GJ input;
- household gas cooking: 1.2 g/GJ input; and
- CCS power: natural gas 0.14, coal 3.4, biomass 133 g/GJ input.

No PM-control credit is inferred for CCS routes because the modeled CO2
capture train does not specify particulate-control equipment.

Agriculture motive diesel uses the guidebook's 1,913 g/Mg-fuel factor and the
retained 43.1 GJ/Mg diesel basis:

`EAR = (1913 / 43.1) × IAR(2.5) × 0.001 = 0.11096287703016242 kt/PJ activity`.

For road transport, model activity is one billion vehicle-kilometres. Thus a
factor in g/vehicle-km has the same numeric value in kt/billion vehicle-km.
Tyre/brake and road-surface wear are summed by class:

- two/three-wheel: 0.0034 + 0.0016 = 0.0050;
- car: 0.0093 + 0.0041 = 0.0134;
- van/light truck: 0.0139 + 0.0057 = 0.0196; and
- bus/heavy truck: 0.0316 + 0.0205 = 0.0521.

These class factors apply to every modeled drivetrain. The six liquid-road
technologies retain their inherited exhaust factor and add non-exhaust wear.
Alternative-fuel and PHEV exhaust is not inferred without fleet and standard
shares.

Domestic coal extraction uses mine plus handling:

`0.005 kg/Mg / 22.1 GJ/Mg = 0.0002262443438914027 kt/PJ`.

Coal imports use receiving-harbour handling only:

`0.3 g/Mg × 0.001 kg/g / 22.1 GJ/Mg = 0.00001357466063348416 kt/PJ`.

## Model mapping

In total, 52 existing technologies are affected: 46 receive their first PM2.5
link and six existing liquid-road series receive an additive non-exhaust
component. Values are installed in mode 1 for `SC_0`; modes 2-30 are zero and
the three policy scenarios inherit through null rows. Companion EACR rows are
zero. The 52 technology-level mappings and 2020/2030/2053 check values are in
`MODEL_MAP.csv`; the complete annual values are in the build snapshot.

## Explicit exclusions

- Agriculture oil and gas heat have no input-fuel link in the inherited model.
- Aviation lacks aircraft/engine, payload, and LTO/CCD activity detail.
- The reviewed rail method does not justify drivetrain-neutral rail wear.
- Hydrogen, ammonia, and aggregate fuel-processing activities have no matched
  factor at the current model boundary.
- Crop-residue/waste burning, cement/process dust, and road resuspension have
  no corresponding explicit activity technology; no residual inventory is
  introduced.

These are coverage boundaries, not zero-emission claims, and are retained in
`GAPS.csv`.

## Reproduction and validation

Run `scripts/apply_philippines_v19_pm25_coverage.py` on a clean copy of the v18
case, then `scripts/validate_philippines_v19_pm25.py`. Validation proves that
only `genData.json` and `RYTEM.json` changed, all non-PM2.5 rows (including
CO2e) are unchanged, all source files match their hashes, all 52 factors reach
generated `data.txt`, and GLPK constructs/checks the full optimization matrix.

The validated candidate solved optimally within the 600-second acceptance
limit (471.112 seconds end-to-end; 351.70 CBC wall-clock seconds; objective
369739249.45411736). Against the untouched v18 comparison, annual CO2e is
unchanged. PM2.5 totals increase from 97.9903 to 120.6157 kt in 2020, 123.1661
to 165.2740 kt in 2030, and 168.0710 to 249.2109 kt in 2053. These increases
are reporting coverage, not a new PM constraint or objective term.
